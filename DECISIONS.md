Shapes: `</ name>`, `< name>`, any whitespace (incl. newline, NEL, U+2028, U+2029) as separator, unterminated `<name …` or `</name …` (name right after `<` or `/`, no space) removed to end of line; a spaced shape is stripped only with its closing `>`, so prose `x < limit` stays. In a patch, opening shapes after an identifier or tolerant/unterminated (`a < b`) stay unless frame or hyphenated; closing shapes go. 21 new vectors | Both implementations run the same vectors. Accepted costs: prose `x < y > z` with both brackets is stripped as a spaced tag; fullwidth punctuation in prose becomes ASCII. Not covered: other NFKC mappings (mathematical alphanumerics, circled letters) in tag names |

# Decisions on the vision (2026-11-06)

Vision: `VISION.md`. Each row was agreed with the owner on 2026-11-06.

| Id | Decision | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | Where a person's enrichment of their agent lives | Outside Parley. The difference between two people's agents comes from each person's own instructions, memory, skills, sessions, and work. Parley verifies identity, delivers, enforces limits, and keeps the record of messages, outcomes, and decisions. It does not build or hold a profile of a person. Two alternatives were considered: Parley guarantees the person's standing context reaches their agent and records the person's answers for later rounds; and Parley builds and holds each person's profile of positions and past decisions | The product stays a channel with a record, not a platform that holds knowledge about people. The premise of the product (section 1 of the vision) must still hold, so rule 2 of the vision forbids the quarantine from stripping the person's own setup. The two alternatives can be reopened as a new row if the premise proves too thin in use |
| V2 | Where the vision is kept and when it is read | `VISION.md` at the repository root, linked from the README and from the repository `CLAUDE.md`, read before any feature, design, or scope decision. Conflicts are resolved by changing the choice or by a new row here | Every decision in this project must follow the same problem statement; a file the agent opens at session start is the cheapest way to make that true |
| V3 | Parley against a shared system of record used as the channel | A shared ticket board, document, or merge request thread is a system of record, not a channel. Parley keeps the agent conversation on the channel and writes only conclusions to the record (vision section 3, rule 9; the five fixed words are defined at the top of the vision). Four differences are recorded, in order of weight: identity and authority (a message acts only as the verified sender, inside a ceiling, in a quarantine; text on a board acts with the reader's full permissions), conversation apart from record (people read conclusions, not the argument), delivery into the live session instead of polling, and a bounded exchange with a round cap and defined ends | Raised by a colleague of the owner who runs two agents over a shared board. The answer "no intermediary" is wrong, because the inbox is an intermediary; the answer is which middle and what it is for. No prediction about the future of trackers or wikis is made: they remain the record |
